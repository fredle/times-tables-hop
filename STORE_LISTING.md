# App Store listing

## App name

Times Tables Hop

(Alternatives if taken: "Hop & Say: Times Tables", "Froggy Times Tables Hop")

## Subtitle

Say the answer, hop the road

## Promotional text

Practise numbers, letters, first words and times tables out loud. Say the answer and the frog hops out of the way of the traffic. No typing needed.

## Description

Times tables practice usually means tapping tiny buttons. Young children can say an answer long before they can type one, so in Times Tables Hop they just say it.

A frog is crossing a busy road. Each lane shows a question and a car starts driving towards the frog. Say the right answer out loud and the frog hops to the next lane. Reach the lily pad and the next level is a little faster.

Four games in one:
- Numbers 1 to 9: say the number you see. Made for reception age.
- Letters: say the letter you see.
- Words: read a simple three-letter word such as cat, dog or sun.
- Times tables: pick any tables from 1 to 12.

Made for children:
- Three speeds, from Relaxed to Speedy.
- A missed question shows the answer and comes back a little later.
- The game only listens while a game is being played.
- No adverts, no accounts, no purchases.
