# Times Tables Hop privacy policy

Times Tables Hop is published by FBL Consulting Ltd. It is made for children
to play, and it has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking
of any kind.

## What stays on the device

The game keeps its settings (which game, which tables, speed) and best scores
on the device. Nothing about play is sent anywhere.

## Speaking answers

When a game is running, the microphone listens for spoken answers. Apple's
speech recognition turns them into text, on the device wherever the device
supports it; otherwise Apple processes the audio under Apple's own privacy
policy. Times Tables Hop does not record, keep or send your voice or what was
said. The microphone is off outside a game.

## Feedback from grown-ups

Behind a "For grown-ups" link, which first asks a sum a young child can't
answer, a grown-up can send the developer a note about what would make the
app better. Only the note, the app's name and version, and the platform are
sent, to the developer's server (feedback.leatham.net). No name, email,
device identifier or location is sent, and the server does not store IP
addresses. Notes are used only to improve the app and are never shared or
sold. Please don't put personal details in a note.

## Children

The app collects no personal information from children. The feedback note is
for grown-ups only and is behind a parental gate.

## Contact and changes

Questions about this policy, or a request to delete a note you sent, can be
raised at https://github.com/fredle/times-tables-hop/issues. If this policy
changes, the new version will be published at this address.
